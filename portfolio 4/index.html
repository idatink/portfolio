Pulse. An ongoing study of how rhythmic light, sound and animation shape perception. It began when I noticed that sitting next to a pulsing light felt like being in company. Each object and sculpture has proximity or touch sensors, and answers the person nearby with a pulse.

Prints. A series inspired by giving my fingerprints at border control in different countries. What would happen if every print had a sound?

How would a fingerprint turn into notes, and then into music?

Time study no. 1. A second-year interaction design brief, answered in thread. Screens and round clocks make time feel endless. This woven calendar is unpicked one thread a day, so you watch the year run out.

Time study no. 2. One year as a single long string, shaped into a dandelion on a kinetic canvas. The mechanism removes one string each day.

A platform for navigating every part of fertility, for women of all ages. Egg freezing, IVF and hormone checks come first: search clinics and tests, with the full cost shown up front.

One search replaces ten browser tabs, and every option can be compared side by side.

Brand design: the name, palette, type and voice.

Letterbird
IndexAbout
2026
UI designMotion design

A voice journal with no typing. Letterbird calls once a day at a time you choose, asks up to three gentle questions, and keeps what you say as a page in your journal. I designed the UI and the motion kit for the Letterbird team.

Paper and linen for the day, ink for the night call, and one grainy meadow green as the only accent. Hand-drawn lines and an uneven sketch border keep it feeling written, not built.

Setup is three screens: pick your questions, pick the evening, and let it call. If you miss it, it tries once more, then lets the day go.

Each state of the call has its own motion: sound arcs while it rings, bars that swell while you talk, a scribble while the page is written, and a sprig that grows when it is saved.

Challenge

Journaling apps ask you to sit down and write, and most people stop within a week.

How I solved it

Take the writing away. One call a day asks the questions and turns your answers into a page, with no streaks to break, just a calendar that fills as you pick up. Inspired by Granola, the voice chat is turned into text in its own style: your words as a written page, not a transcript.

Image engine

We also built an image-processing engine. It reads what the patient hopes for, compares their photo uploads with it, and shows suitable results and example after-surgery photos.

Challenge

Patients need to feel emotionally supported and genuinely safe, and that safety has to be real, not just reassuring words in a chat.

How I solved it

We studied patient journeys that had gone wrong and designed every step to prevent those failures, so safety is built into the service itself.

Connectd
IndexAbout
2024
Lead product design

A marketplace matching startups with the advisors and fractional leaders they need to grow. It launched three-sided, with investors too. My research showed why to drop them, and the leadership team agreed: it is now two-sided.

connectd.co >

Fewer sides, better matches: removing investors let the product focus on startups and advisors, and on trust between them.

Challenge

Three user types with different goals made the matching feel noisy. Onboarding and retention numbers were weak, and founders churned soon after signing up.

How I solved it

Research showed investors diluted the experience, so we cut to two sides and rebuilt onboarding around startup–advisor fit: an educational onboarding inspired by Airbnb, with ongoing in-product support.
